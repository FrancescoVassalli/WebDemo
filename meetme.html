Meet Lauren

Hello, Arvada! I’m Lauren Simpson, and I’m proud to serve as your mayor.

My story in Arvada began with a lunch in Olde Town. My husband, Steven, and I were looking for a place to put down roots and raise a family. We visited Arvada in November 2013, fell in love with the community before we had even finished our meal, and made an offer on a house that very same day. We weren’t looking for somewhere to simply live. We had found somewhere we wanted to belong.

Civic Service

At the time, I never imagined I’d one day serve as Arvada’s mayor. My professional career was in international relations and diplomacy. I studied at Baylor University and Catholic University, worked in public affairs, and spent eight years serving as a senior foreign policy and diplomatic officer with the Consulate General of Canada in Denver.

But I discovered something I hadn’t expected: some of the most meaningful work I could do wasn’t happening overseas or in Washington. It was happening right here at home. I began volunteering, organizing neighbors, and eventually running for City Council in 2019. Helping people with everyday problems, bringing people together, and making a tangible difference in my community became the most rewarding work of my life.

I have always believed that good leadership requires more than having the loudest voice in the room. It requires listening, building relationships, finding common ground, and being willing to do the hard work of getting things done.

Your Mayor

As mayor, I’ve worked alongside residents, local businesses, nonprofits, and city employees to make progress on the issues that matter to our community. We’ve strengthened our response to homelessness, expanded housing opportunities, invested in roads and essential infrastructure, supported local businesses, and continued creating public spaces where our community can come together. In 2025 alone, Arvada welcomed more than 120 new businesses, while our bridge-housing partnership helped more than 150 people experiencing homelessness, with approximately 70% moving on to permanent housing.

With your help I pledge to continue this important work. We must continue addressing affordability by expanding housing options and investing responsibly in our infrastructure.

Outside City Hall, I’m a wife, a mom, and the executive director of The Feline Fix, a local cat rescue and welfare organization. Whether I’m helping a neighbor navigate a difficult situation, working with community partners, or caring for animals who need a second chance, I believe that service is about showing up when people and communities need you.

It has given me an extraordinary opportunity to serve. I’m running for re-election because I believe in this community, I’m proud of what we’ve accomplished together, and I know we have so much more we can achieve.
